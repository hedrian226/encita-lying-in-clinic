About Encita

Care that feels like family

Encita Lying-in Clinic sits in Parklane, Country Homes Subdivision, right in the heart of Barangay San Francisco, General Trias. We're a neighborhood clinic by design — small enough to know you by name, equipped enough to see you through pregnancy, delivery, and your baby's first checkups.

We're PhilHealth-partnered and DOH-accredited, with our own birthing home, ultrasound, and laboratory on-site — plus a visiting pediatrician and general OPD services, so growing families in General Trias have one trusted place to turn to, before, during, and after delivery.

What we offer

Every stage of your journey, in one clinic

Our services follow the path most families walk with us — from your first checkup to your child's pediatric visits.

Step 1 · Prenatal

OB-GYN Checkups & Ultrasound

Regular prenatal consultations to track you and your baby's health, with on-site ultrasound and laboratory testing so results come back fast — no need to travel elsewhere.

Prenatal checkups On-site ultrasound On-site laboratory PhilHealth-covered visits
Step 2 · Delivery

Midwife-Attended, OB-Supervised Delivery

Deliver with a team that already knows your prenatal history — our midwives attend delivery with OB-GYN oversight, right in our own birthing home, with no balance billing for qualified low-risk pregnancies.

Midwife-attended birth OB supervision No Balance Billing*
Step 3 · Newborn

Pediatric & Well-Baby Care

Our pediatric and well-baby services support your newborn's checkups from day one. Provider schedules may change, so please confirm availability before visiting.

Well-baby checkups Schedule updated by clinic

Contraceptive Counseling & OPD

One-on-one guidance to help you choose the family planning method that fits your life, plus general outpatient (OPD) consultations for the whole family.
