How to manage Appointments

Purpose: Requests submitted by patients on the public website appear here. Move each request through Pending → Confirmed → Completed. Cancel or reopen a request when needed.

Important: A website request is not automatically confirmed. Clinic staff should contact the patient before treating it as a confirmed appointment.

Purpose: Update the current or upcoming provider schedule shown to visitors. Use the title, provider, specialty, time text and note to keep patients informed.

Best practice: If availability changes often, keep wording general and ask patients to call or message the clinic to confirm.

How to use News

Purpose: Publish permanent or long-lived content such as clinic updates, health tips, Mom & Baby information, service updates, schedules and community news. Visitors can open a post to read the full content.

Use Announcements instead for urgent or temporary notices such as closures, holiday hours or last-minute provider changes.

Add / edit news

Use News for permanent website content such as clinic updates, health tips, schedules and community news. For urgent temporary notices, use the separate Announcements tab.

News posts

How to use Announcements

Purpose: Publish important or temporary clinic notices. Start/end times control when a notice is relevant, and priority helps determine which active notice is emphasized.

Examples: Clinic closure, holiday hours, urgent schedule changes or temporary provider availability. Use News for regular informational posts.

Urgent announcement banner

Use Announcements only for urgent or temporary notices. You can set start/end times and priority; the notice appears in the website banner and Updates feed while active.

How to manage the Care Team

Purpose: Add public-facing profiles for doctors, midwives and approved clinic staff. Include the name, role, specialty, schedule, bio and a professional photo.

Where it appears: Published profiles are shown in the public Care Team section. Optimize large photos before uploading for faster mobile loading.

How to manage Services

Purpose: Add extra services to the separate public “Our Services” gallery with a picture, category, name, description and availability. Published services can also be available in the appointment request options.

Note: The original four care-journey cards remain separate and are not replaced by CMS services.
